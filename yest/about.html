About Us

Chakrin

Chakrin Advanced Research Systems Pvt. Ltd. is a technology-driven engineering company engaged in the design, development, integration and indigenization of advanced electronic, RF, power, embedded and mechanical systems for demanding and critical applications.

Our multidisciplinary engineering approach brings together expertise across electronics, RF and microwave engineering, power electronics, embedded systems, mechanical design, thermal management and system integration.

We work with customers to transform complex technical requirements into functional, reliable, testable and manufacturable engineering solutions.

From concept development and system architecture through detailed design, simulation, prototyping, testing, qualification and manufacturing support, Chakrin provides an integrated engineering development ecosystem.

Vision

Building Technology For A Self-Reliant Future

To build a technology-driven engineering organization that develops and indigenizes advanced systems for India and the global technology ecosystem.
